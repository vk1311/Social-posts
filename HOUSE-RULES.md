# House rules for every brand

These apply to Keegix, Staffkin, Waypoint and JJ Pumps. Each brand bible adds its own rules on top.

1. **No invented reviews, testimonials, quotes, user counts or logos of customers.** Use real ones with real names, or leave the space labelled "pending".
2. **Mechanisms, not outcomes.** "Edit once, nothing to reprint" or "day-7 reminder" is fine. "Save 5 hours a week" or "+42% bookings" is not, unless it has been tested and documented first (Canada's Competition Act).
3. **Competitors:** don't name them in ads. If you ever do, only state facts checked that day on their own public page, and never use their logos.
4. **Stats need a source.** Any number about the market (e.g. "735 hours…", "54% of HR professionals…") must have its source written in the post notes.
5. **Money:** write CAD after every Canadian price. JJ Pumps uses INR, or no prices at all.
6. **Spelling:** Canadian English for the Canadian brands (colour, centre, labour, neighbour). JJ Pumps uses Indian English and a respectful, festival-aware tone.
7. **No hype words:** revolutionary, seamless, unlock, game-changer, 10x.
8. **Mock-ups use made-up data:** fake names, 555 phone numbers and fake passwords. Never a real client's data, a personal email, or a live admin URL.
9. **Music:** only tracks cleared for business use (the Edits or Meta business library). Business pages that use popular songs get muted or blocked.
10. **Free tiers first.** Paid tools and ads only once a brand has real users to optimise against.
11. **Privacy:** no personal cell numbers on public pages unless intended. Business numbers only: Waypoint 902-670-9297, JJ Pumps +91 92271 06108.
