For the five videos in `jj/reels/videos/`. These replace `jj/reels/voiceover-scripts-english-SUPERSEDED.txt`.

**How to use**
- Voice: male, 35–50, calm and clear, like a pump mechanic explaining across the counter (`jj/video/editor-brief.md`).
- Times are seconds into the video. I matched them to what's on screen in each reel (I pulled frames every 1–2 seconds).
- Each reel ends on the fixed closing line: **"Call ya WhatsApp karein: 92271 06108. Jay Jalaram Pumps. Water Power You Can Depend On."** It takes about 4.5 seconds at a calm pace.
- On-screen captions: Hinglish, Roman script, word for word with the voice, Poppins Bold, max 2 lines, outside the safe areas (top 250 px, bottom 420 px, right 140 px).
- Music: royalty-free, cleared for business use, instrumental, 20–30% under the voice.
- No numbers are spoken in any script. Facts used: copper rotor motors, made in our own Ahmedabad workshop, product and service names, plus plain how-it-works lines.
- Captions below are in English (the bible says social captions stay English for now).

**Posting slots.** The Tue/Sat 10:00 Halifax slots (7:30 PM India time) are taken by batch-3 posts from Nov 14 to Dec 15, so I suggest Thursdays at the same time. That gives three posts a week, which matches the bible's cadence.
