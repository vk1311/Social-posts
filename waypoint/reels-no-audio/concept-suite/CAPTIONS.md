## Post captions (drafts)

**S00 · Suite overview**
One customer, one record, and five apps that each pick up where the last one stopped: the booking, the crew's job, the invoice, the pay run and Monday's numbers. Every app in this suite is a concept with made-up data. I build only the ones your business needs, around how you already work. waypointns.ca

**S01 · Clients**
Every call, quote, job and invoice for one customer, on one timeline. Add "gate code 4471, dog in the back yard" once, and the crew sees it on every job at that address. Book the next visit from the same page. This is a concept demo with made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S02 · Schedule**
A fall cleanup request comes in online. The office sees the whole week and which crew is already working on that street, then confirms with one tap. The customer gets a text and the crew gets the job. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S03 · Quotes & invoicing**
Quote Q-1042 for a Kentville driveway takes two taps from the saved price list, and HST adds itself ($820.80 CAD). It goes out as a link and gets signed. One tap turns it into an invoice. The e-transfer is matched by amount and memo before anyone marks it Paid. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S04 · Jobs (crew app)**
The office adds "Gate code 4471. Dog in the back yard." once. The crew lead sees it pinned on the job. He ticks the checklist, adds before and after photos, and marks it done. His hours are logged, and the invoice is drafted from the same job. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S05 · People (onboarding)**
A new hire fills in his TD1s, banking details and WHMIS training from his own phone. The office hands over boots and a radio and ticks that item off itself. Once the profile is complete, Payroll already has him. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S06 · Payroll**
Crew hours come straight from the job app. A missed clock-out gets flagged and fixed with a note. Every deduction is listed for review, and nothing goes out until the owner taps approve. Then each person gets their stub on their phone. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S07 · Time off**
A time-off request shows up next to that week's crew calendar, with who else is off and what's already booked. Approve it, the balance updates, and the schedule works around those days. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S08 · Helpdesk**
First snowfall, and a customer texts that the plow missed the end of his driveway. The text is already linked to his client record and last night's route stop. The office assigns it to the crew lead, edits a saved reply and sends it. When the crew marks the revisit done, the ticket closes. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S09 · Stock**
Every driveway the crew salts takes bags off the ice melt count. Below the reorder point, a purchase order is drafted, and nothing goes to the supplier until the owner approves it. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S10 · Campaigns**
The client list already knows who had a fall cleanup and still has no snow contract. Pick them, and only the clients with consent on file are kept (Canada's anti-spam law, CASL). Write the email once and schedule it for Tuesday morning. Replies land in Helpdesk. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca

**S11 · Reports**
Monday morning, last week's numbers are already filled in: jobs done, invoices sent, what's unpaid and crew hours, each pulled from the app where the work happened. Tap "unpaid" and reminders are drafted, but nothing goes out until you send it. Concept demo, made-up numbers. waypointns.ca

**S12 · Assistant**
"Who still owes us from September?" Ask it in plain words. The answer comes from your own invoices, with the sources shown, and the assistant drafts the reminders. Nothing goes out until you approve each one. AI drafts, a person decides. Concept demo, made-up data. waypointns.ca
