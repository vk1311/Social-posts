## What Waypoint is
Waypoint (Waypoint Software, Nova Scotia; waypointns.ca) builds custom software for small service and trades businesses (quotes, agreements, signatures, routes, invoices, receipts: "one place"). Positioning: "I stopped selling readymade. I build software — full systems". Waypoint Labs is the product arm, which includes Keegix and the other side tools.

## Content rules: do not change these (from NOTES.md)
1. **Every figure is a mechanism, never an outcome.** For example "~30 sec to send an invoice" or "day 7 reminder", never "+42% bookings" (Competition Act).
2. **No invented testimonials.** The three cards on /work stay labelled "pending" until there are real quotes and real names.
3. **No ranking promises.** Nobody can promise the top slot; what is achievable is being readable.
4. **Data residency means "Canada", not "Nova Scotia".**
5. **DJ Movers is named on /work only.** Elsewhere it's "a Valley moving company".

## Voice (from the posts)
Seasonal, trades-aware humour: frost on the windshield, plow routes, the coffee ring on the invoicing system, Thanksgiving and the truck. It's first-person founder talk, and it's honest about competitors ("Jobber is genuinely good software, and if their way of running…").
