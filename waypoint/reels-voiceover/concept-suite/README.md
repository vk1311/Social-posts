# Waypoint concept suite: reels with voiceover

The same 13 reels with a narrator voiceover, in two voices:
- `bella/`: Kokoro **af_bella** (American female)
- `michael/`: Kokoro **am_michael** (American male)

Kokoro TTS v1.0 is the same engine Snortlo uses, and it is Apache 2.0, so commercial use is fine. The music is ducked to about 30% under the voice, and the tap and notification sounds stay. Loudness is about −16 LUFS.

Every line was checked with speech recognition (Whisper) and lands inside its caption window.

**When posting on Instagram or Facebook, turn on the AI label.** Meta asks for it on AI-generated voices. The narrator is written in the third person on purpose, because the voice isn't Vatsal.

Silent versions are in `../../reels-no-audio/concept-suite/`, and music-only versions are in `../../reels-with-audio/concept-suite/`. Captions are in `../../reels-no-audio/concept-suite/CAPTIONS.md`.

To re-make (needs `kokoro-onnx` and the Kokoro model files): `python3 vo.py af_bella am_michael` in `reels-no-audio/concept-suite/source/`.
