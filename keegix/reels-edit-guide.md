# Keegix reels: edit guide

There are 6 reels. Each is 1080×1920, silent, and already has its on-screen text, so music alone works. A voiceover is optional.
Each reel's caption and cover image sit next to its video.

## Posting schedule (Keegix Facebook, 7:00 pm Atlantic)

These dates fill the gaps between the image posts that are already scheduled.

| File | Post date | Length | Audio plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01_Thu-08-Oct_launch | Thu Oct 8 | 24.5 s | Music + voiceover |
| 02_Thu-15-Oct_setup | Thu Oct 15 | 19.5 s | Music + your voiceover |
| 03_Tue-20-Oct_1am-thermostat | Tue Oct 20 | 16.5 s | Music + sound effects, no voiceover |

**About the music:** Keegix posts from a business page, so use tracks from the audio library in Edits that are cleared for business use (Meta's own library). A mainstream song can get the video muted or blocked, especially if you post it through Postiz.

**When you're done:** export each one at 1080×1920 and keep the same file names. Upload them to the Social-posts repo on GitHub (any folder), tell me, and I'll schedule them in Postiz on these dates. Or post them from Edits yourself at these times.

Keep music around 20–30% volume under a voiceover. Keep all voiceover lines within the times given, because the on-screen text changes at those points.
