## 1. What Keegix is

**One line:** A welcome book your guests open on their phone.
**Tagline:** Let the house answer the questions.

Keegix gives short-term rental hosts one guest link and one printable QR sign. The guide covers check-in, wifi, parking, appliances, house rules, trash day, local picks, emergency contacts and the checkout list. Guests open it in any phone browser, with no app and no account.

### Who it's for
- Hosts who don't live next door to the property
- Cottage and cabin owners with a season rather than year-round bookings
- Co-hosts and small property managers running 1–20 units
- Anyone whose house manual is a Word doc in a kitchen drawer

**First market:** Cape Breton and Nova Scotia hosts. NS requires every short-term rental to register, so hosts are findable. The season runs June to October, and hosts buy tools February to April.
**Later:** global, including South America, Europe, South Africa, Newfoundland and the rest of tourist Canada.

### What it deliberately doesn't do
No bookings, no payments, no calendar sync, no channel management. It sits beside Airbnb, not against it. Say this proudly. It's a positioning line, not an apology.

### Pricing (always in CAD, always say CAD)
| Plan | Price | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 property, all sections, reordering, QR sign, up to 5 photos, optional guest password, small "Made with Keegix" footer |
| Pro | $9 per property per month | As many properties as you pay for, no footer, custom colours for the guest page and sign, unlimited photos, cancel any time from the billing page |
| Set up for you | $39 one time | "I build one property from your notes", up to 5 photos, link and QR sign ready, one round of changes within 14 days, lands on the free plan |

No card is needed to start.

### Real features you can talk about (and nothing else)
- One guest link per property, plus a letter-size QR sign as a PDF. Edits show up without reprinting.
- Sections: check-in, wifi, parking, appliances, house rules, trash day, local picks, emergency contacts, checkout list, and your own sections. Rename, hide and reorder them.
- A photo on any section.
- Wifi: a copy button on the network and password, plus a "Show wifi QR code" so other devices can join.
- "Open in Maps" on addresses.
- Save as PDF for places with no signal, plus a separate one-page checkout card.
- An optional guest password.
- Pro: custom accent colour and no footer.

If a post needs a feature that isn't on this list, check the product first.

## 2. Logo

**The mark:** a key tag, rounded on top and pointed at the bottom, with a buoy-yellow hole and two lines (a label). It reads as a key tag and a map pin at once.
**The wordmark:** "Keegix" in Archivo ExtraBold, set wide (font-stretch 118%). Capital K.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `brand/keegix-mark.svg` | Navy mark for light backgrounds |
| `brand/keegix-mark-white.svg` | White mark for navy or photo backgrounds |
| `brand/logo-lockup-navy.png` / `-white.png` | Mark + wordmark |
| `brand/profile-picture-1080.png` | Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn avatar. Safe inside the circle crop |
| `brand/facebook-cover-1640x856.png` | Facebook cover. Text is kept high and centred so the round avatar doesn't cover it on phones |
| `brand/qr-keegix-com.png` | A QR code to keegix.com for marketing. Never use a real guest property's QR in marketing |

**Rules**
- Clear space: at least half the mark's width on every side.
- Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen.
- Don't recolour, stretch, outline, add shadows, or put it on a busy photo without a solid panel or dark gradient behind it.
- **Retired:** the house-with-keyhole logo (`listings/OLD-logo-house-keyhole-DO-NOT-USE.png`). Replace it anywhere it still appears, including LinkedIn.

## 3. Colour

The palette is taken straight from keegix.com. Its Maritime inspiration: harbour at night, a yellow buoy, sea glass.

| Name | Hex | Use |

| Harbour navy | `#15304A` | Primary. Text, headers, dark backgrounds, buttons |
| Deep navy | `#0E2236` | Night scenes, darkest panels |

| Buoy yellow (pressed) | `#E8B516` | Hover and pressed states |
| Sea glass | `#E3ECE9` | Light section backgrounds |

| Rule | `#D3DCDA` | Lines and dividers |
| Slate | `#4E5C66` | Secondary text |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, page background |

**Guest page default accent** (free plan): `#1F4D3A` green. Marketing mock-ups use navy because Pro hosts can pick their own colour.

**Ratios that work:** about 60% sand, white or sea glass, 30% navy, 10% yellow. Never put yellow text on white, and never put yellow on sea glass for body text.

## 4. Type

Both fonts are free on Google Fonts.

| Font | Where | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Newsreader** (serif) | Big headlines, taglines, emotional lines | Weight 500, letter-spacing −0.025em, tight leading (1.0–1.05). Use italic for the punchline or second line, often in buoy yellow on navy |
| **Archivo** | Everything else: UI, labels, prices, buttons, the wordmark | Bold/ExtraBold for labels and headings in UI, set wide (font-stretch 112–125%); Regular for body |

- **Kickers:** Archivo ExtraBold, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em, font-stretch 125%, small. For example "MEET KEEGIX" or "PRICING · CANADIAN DOLLARS".
- **Sentence case** everywhere except kickers.

## 5. Voice

**A calm, dry-humoured host friend. Maritime, practical, honest.**

**Do**
- Be specific: the fussy shower, which bin goes out Thursday, the 11:47 PM text, the thermostat with four buttons.
- Write short sentences with plain words.
- Say what it doesn't do. Let the binder win a round.
- Use first person for founder pieces ("Send me your notes. I'll build it.").
- Use Canadian spelling (colour, neighbour, favourite), and write CAD after every price.
- Use humour about the situation, never about guests or hosts.

**Don't**
- Invent reviews, testimonials, user counts or "trusted by X hosts". There are no paying hosts yet.
- Make outcome claims ("save 5 hours a week"). State mechanisms instead ("edit once, nothing to reprint").
- Use hype words: revolutionary, seamless, unlock, game-changer, 10x.
- Name competitors in ads or posts. Say "the binder", "a PDF by email" or "some guidebook apps". If you ever do name one, only state facts you checked that day on their public page, and never use their logo (Competition Act).
- Say "the only free option". Hostfully has a free tier too.

**Words we use:** welcome book, guest link, QR sign, host, guests, property, sections.
**Words we avoid:** guidebook app (in our own copy), platform, solution, leverage, users (say hosts or guests).

### Copy bank
- Let the house answer the questions.
- The welcome book your guests open on their phone.
- Check-in, wifi, parking and checkout. One guest link and one QR sign.
- Edit it once. Nothing to reprint.
- No app. No account. Any phone.
- Free for one property. No card needed to start.
- Put it on the QR sign. Go back to sleep.
- Keep the binder. It makes a great doorstop.
- Answered before they asked.

Descriptions at 25, 50, 100 and 250 words are in `listings/keegix-asset-kit.md`.

## 6. Visual style for posts

- **Formats:** Instagram and Facebook images and carousels at 1080×1350 (4:5). Reels at 1080×1920. Keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px of reels.
- **Photography:** Maritime cottages: grey cedar shingles, coloured doors, porches, sea views, golden or dusky light. Warm, lived-in, no people's faces up close. The photo library is in `photos/`.
- **Motifs:** the guest page on a phone, the QR sign on a door or in a frame, the coffee-stained binder as the recurring villain, text-message bubbles, lock screens.
- **Layout habits:** a big Newsreader headline top left, the logo and page dots in the footer, one yellow accent, lots of air.
- **Carousels:** slide 1 is the hook, the last slide is the CTA on buoy yellow or navy, and there are 5–7 slides.
- **Mock-ups:** show only real features, with made-up data (Harbour Street Cottage, a 555 phone number, fake wifi passwords).
- **Reels:** they're rendered silent. Add music from the Edits or Meta library, cleared for business. A voiceover is optional. See `reels-edit-guide.md`.

`design-templates.zip` holds the HTML templates every post was built from (render with Python and Playwright; they expect the photos in an `assets/` folder beside them). Use them so new posts come out consistent.
