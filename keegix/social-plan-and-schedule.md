## How to schedule everything in one sitting (free)

1. Open **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) in your browser and choose the Keegix page. Instagram needs to be connected to the Facebook page first: **Settings > Linked accounts > Instagram**.
2. Click **Planner** in the left menu, then **Create post** (or **Create reel** for the videos).
3. Tick both **Facebook** and **Instagram** at the top.
4. Upload the files from the post's folder. For carousels, upload `slide-01` to `slide-07` in order.
5. Paste the Instagram caption and hashtags. Business Suite lets you switch to **Customize for each platform**, so paste the Facebook caption there.
6. Add the alt text: **Advanced settings > Alt text**.
7. Click the arrow beside **Publish** and choose **Schedule**. Pick the date and time from the table.
8. Repeat for all 16. After each one goes live, post its first comment yourself from your phone.

**Reels:** the videos are silent on purpose, because baked-in music can get a reel muted. Business Suite doesn't add Instagram music, so to add sound, post that reel from the Instagram app instead: **+ > Reel**, pick the video, **Audio**, choose a track at low volume, then **Share**. Choose `cover.jpg` as the cover image.
