## Rules that apply to every brand

1. **No invented numbers.** No made-up stats, percentages or survey figures. A number is fine only if the reader can check it (a price, a date, a count, or a figure with its source linked).
2. **No fake proof.** No invented testimonials, reviews, client logos, user counts or "time saved" claims. Empty review slots stay labelled "pending".
3. **Say what it does, not what it will earn.** Mechanisms, not outcomes. No search-ranking promises.
4. **No competitor names or logos.** No platform logos either; platform names as plain text only.
5. **Data residency is "Canada".** Not a province, not a city.
6. **No engagement bait.** No "comment YES", no "tag a friend", no comment-to-get offers.
7. **Real photos only.** No AI-generated images for any brand. Staffkin uses real photos or type only.
8. **News hooks quote the real headline,** with the outlet and date, and link the source.
9. **Staffkin legal or news posts end with "Not legal advice."**
10. **Mock-ups use made-up data.** Never a real person's or client's details.
11. **No hype words.** Not "revolutionary", "seamless", "unlock", "game-changer", "10x".
12. **Prices in CAD** for Waypoint, Keegix and Staffkin. JJ Pumps shows no prices.
13. **Brand accounts use the brand mark** as the profile picture, never a personal photo.
