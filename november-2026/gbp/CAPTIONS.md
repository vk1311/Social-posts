## JJ Pumps · 04-nov19-pm-kusum · 2026-11-19T14:00Z
Image: `jj-04-nov19-pm-kusum.jpg` · Button: Call

"MNRE Extends PM-KUSUM Financial Closure Deadline to Nov 30" (Energetica India, Oct 1, 2026)

PM-KUSUM is the central scheme for solar in agriculture. Component B covers standalone solar pumps. Component C covers solarising existing grid-connected farm pumps. The new Nov 30, 2026 date is for financial closure under Components A and C. The project completion deadline is still 31 March 2027.

Thinking about solar for your farm? Ask your DISCOM or your state's nodal agency about current eligibility before you buy anything.

Questions about your borewell pump? We're happy to help.
