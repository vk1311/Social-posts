## What Staffkin is
**Tagline:** AI does the paperwork. People make the hire.
A hiring and onboarding kit for small Canadian employers: a semi-ATS with job descriptions, interview guides and scoring, reference checks and onboarding, with AI drafting. The brand should feel steady, warm and plain-spoken, like a tool a restaurant owner trusts on a busy Tuesday.
- **Upsell:** Staffkin HR Suite (a basic tool at first, a full suite on request, sold by booking a demo).
- **Newer message:** the Staffkin HR Agent, in early access. It works overnight on deadlines, letters and reminders, and you approve everything.
- **Market:** Canada first, with global intent (South America, Europe, Newfoundland, South Africa).

## Logo
- **The mark:** two linked rings, meaning two people, one team.
- **The wordmark:** always lowercase **staffkin**.
- **Clear space:** the height of one ring on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 24 px tall on screen, 8 mm in print.
- Use the mark alone for app icons and profile pictures.

## Type
**Outfit** (Google Fonts) is used for everything. Headings 600, labels and buttons 500, body 400. Sentence case always.

## Voice
- Say what it does, for example "One click emails the candidate for consent."
- Plain Canadian English (behaviour, centre, labour).
- Human first: AI writes drafts; people score and decide.
- Honest numbers only: prices and mechanisms, never promised results.
- Never use "revolutionary", "seamless", "unlock" or "10x".
- No invented testimonials or time-saved claims.
- Don't recolour, stretch, outline or shadow the logo, and don't put it on busy photos without a solid panel.
