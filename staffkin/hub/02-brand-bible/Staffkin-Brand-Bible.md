## Logo and mark

The logo is two linked rings (two people, one team) beside the lowercase wordmark.

| Version | Rings | Use on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full logo, standard | Indigo + periwinkle rings, indigo wordmark | White or Paper backgrounds |
| Full logo, white | White + periwinkle rings, white wordmark | Indigo or dark backgrounds |
| Mark only, standard | Indigo + periwinkle | Favicons, light icons |
| Mark only, white | White + periwinkle | Profile pictures, app icon on indigo, Stripe icon |

**Rules**
- Clear space around the logo equals the height of one ring.
- Smallest size: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print.
- Use the mark alone for icons and profile pictures.
- Never recolour, stretch, outline or shadow the logo.
- Never put it on a busy photo without a solid panel behind it.

**End cards**
- **Hiring:** indigo background, white and periwinkle rings, "Hire people, not paperwork.", a "Try for free" button, staffkin.com, and "No card needed · Made in Nova Scotia".
- **HR Agent:** light background, rings, "Staffkin HR Agent", "You're there for your people. We handle the sheets and systems.", and "Book a demo at staffkin.com/hr-agent".

**Website hero:** a faded real photo on one side and large rings peeking in from the edge (about 60% visible, about 20% opacity). Readability wins over decoration.

## Voice and messaging

Say what it does, in plain Canadian English: people first, honest numbers only. A tool a restaurant owner trusts on a busy Tuesday.

**Tone rules**
- **Say what it does:** "One click emails the candidate for consent."
- **Plain Canadian English:** behaviour, centre, labour. Short sentences.
- **People first:** AI drafts; people score, decide and approve.
- **Warm and calm,** never hype. Humour is specific and observational.

**Key messages, hiring**
1. Hire people, not paperwork.
2. Your first job is free, no card needed.
3. Reference checks for $9, no subscription.
4. Structured interviews with a 1–5 scoring guide, so candidates are compared fairly.
5. Canadian paperwork built in: TD1, WHMIS, direct deposit.

**Key messages, HR Agent**
1. You're there for your people. We handle the sheets and systems.
2. It works overnight, and you approve in the morning.
3. Every HR date is tracked, and you hear about it before it's due.
4. Letters are written for the employee's province. Nothing is sent without you.
5. It covers all 13 provinces and territories, plus federal.

**Hard rules**
- No invented statistics. Only these two can be used, each with its source:
  - 54% of HR professionals burned out in the past five years (Personio survey of 500 HR professionals, via People Management, Jan 2025).
  - Canadian small businesses spent 735 hours on regulatory compliance in 2024 (CFIB, Canada's Red Tape Report, Jan 2025).
- No testimonials, customer names, reviews, time-saved claims or promised results.
- Don't use these words: revolutionary, seamless, unlock, game-changer, 10x.
- No engagement bait (tag someone, comment YES).
- **In hiring, AI only drafts questions.** It never reads resumes, never scores or rejects anyone.
- **The HR Agent drafts, tracks and flags.** It never decides, never scores or ranks people, and never sends on its own.
- No prices for the HR Agent. It's early access, so the call to action is "Book a demo".
- Wage figures only when checked against a current source.
- Contact email stays hello@waypointns.ca.
- No social handles in content for now.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- Prices are in Canadian dollars. Yearly billing costs 10 months.
- **Add-ons:**
  - 1 reference check: $9
  - 5 reference checks: $35
  - 1 AI guide: $4
  - 50 AI credits: $5 (paid plans)

**Hiring tools, what you get:**
- Scoring guides for 78 roles and free job description templates for 70 roles.
- Prescreens go out by email, and answers come back in one place.
- Candidates are compared side by side.
- Reference checks with the candidate's consent first, plus a PDF report.
- Onboarding checklist covering TD1, direct deposit and WHMIS.
- Candidates and referees don't need an account.

**HR Agent, what it does:**
- Tracks probation ends, certification expiries, leave returns, anniversaries and last days.
- Drafts letters for the employee's province: probation extensions, warnings, offers, leave approvals and return-to-work plans.
- Puts every draft in an approval queue, where you approve, edit or bin it.
- Covers all 13 provinces and territories plus federally regulated employers.
- Shows its sources and flags anything it isn't sure of.
- Gives a final pay and ROE checklist when someone leaves.
- Gets rules updates when employment standards change.
- Gives each company its own private install, with records stored in Canada.

## Video rules

- **Format:** vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, MP4. The YouTube walkthrough is the only 16:9 video.
- **Look:** motion graphics and product screens only. No faces on camera, and real photos only (never AI-generated).
- **Voice-over:**
  - Female, Canadian accent, warm and calm.
  - About 2.4 words per second (a 21 s reel is about 50 words).
  - Structure: hook → story → hook → story.
- **Captions:**
  - The main captions are already burned into each video. Don't add auto-captions on top.
  - The narration must mean the same as the on-screen text.
- **Music:** royalty-free and cleared for business use, at 15–20% under the voice-over.
  - Hiring: light and upbeat.
  - HR Agent: calm piano or ambient.
- **Safe areas:** keep added elements out of the top 220 px, the bottom 420 px and the right 120 px.
- **End card:** don't cover it. Say the call to action as it appears.

## Channels, partners and rules

**Acquisition, cheapest first**
1. SEO pages, with one new page a week.
2. The $9 reference check, the easiest first purchase: staffkin.com/app/#/signup?intent=refs
3. Free-plan branding on every email a free user sends.
4. Free software directories and Canadian small-business listings.
5. Partners: bookkeepers, accountants, payroll providers, HR consultants.
6. Associations: restaurant (e.g. RANS), continuing care, and chambers of commerce.

**Partner links:** staffkin.com/?ref=CODE, the same format as Keegix. The code is kept for 90 days and applied at checkout as the partner's Stripe promo code.

**Sister tools:** Keegix (welcome books for short-term rental hosts). It links to Staffkin's cleaner hiring pages, and Staffkin's cleaner, housekeeper and custodian pages mention Keegix. Enterprise enquiries become Waypoint custom-build leads.

**Marketing rules**
- No promo posts in local community Facebook groups.
- Every stat has a source.
- Never use real client or prospect names.
