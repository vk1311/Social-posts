# Staffkin: video editor brief

This brief is for the current batch of 14 Staffkin videos. It covers both product lines: **Staffkin hiring tools** and the **Staffkin HR Agent**.

---

## 1. What it is

- **Staffkin** is an AI HR platform for Canadian employers.
  - **Hiring tools:** interview guides with scoring, prescreens, $9 reference checks and onboarding checklists, for small employers who hire a few times a year.
  - **Staffkin HR Agent (early access):** an AI virtual HR agent. It tracks every HR deadline, drafts letters and checklists for each employee's province, and puts everything in an approval queue. Nothing is sent without the employer's approval.
- **Website:** staffkin.com. The HR Agent page is staffkin.com/hr-agent.
- **Social handles:** leave them out. Don't say or show any handle.
- **Made in:** Nova Scotia, Canada. Data is stored in Canada.

## 3. Audience

- **Hiring videos (H-xx):**
  - Who: owners and managers of Canadian businesses with 1–50 staff and no HR person, such as restaurants, trades, retail, care homes and small offices.
  - Situation: they hire a few times a year, often in a rush, using resumes, phone calls and spreadsheets.
  - What they want: to hire faster without the admin.
- **HR Agent videos (HR-xx):**
  - Who: owners, office managers and HR generalists at Canadian employers of any size, often a one-person HR function.
  - Situation: they're buried in deadlines, letters, forms and rule changes that differ by province, and many are close to burnout.
  - What they want: to be there for their people, not their paperwork.

## 4. Voice and tone

- **Voice-over:** a female voice with a Canadian accent. Warm, calm and conversational, like a helpful colleague, not an announcer.
- **Language:** plain Canadian English, with Canadian spelling in any on-screen text (behaviour, centre, labour).
- **Pace:** unhurried. For HR Agent videos, slightly slower and softer.
- **Script shape:** hook → story → hook → story, about 50 words for ~21 s. Most current videos are 14–18 s, so aim for about 2.4 words per second (35–45 words).
- **Match the screen:** narration must mean the same as the text on screen. It can add a little, but it can never contradict it or run ahead of it.

### Hard rules

1. **No invented statistics.** Only these two stats can be used, and only on HR Agent videos:
   - "54% of HR professionals burned out in the past five years." Source: Personio survey of 500 HR professionals, reported by People Management, Jan 2025.
   - "Canadian small businesses spent 735 hours on regulatory compliance in 2024." Source: CFIB, Canada's Red Tape Report, Jan 2025.
2. **No testimonials, customer names, reviews or "trusted by" claims.**
3. **No time-saved or results promises**, like "save 10 hours a week" or "hire better people".
4. **No hype words:** revolutionary, seamless, unlock, game-changer, 10x, supercharge.
5. **No engagement bait:** "comment below", "tag someone", "smash that like".
6. **No jargon:** say "interview guide", not "structured behavioural assessment framework".
7. **AI stays honest:**
   - In hiring, AI only drafts interview questions. It never reads resumes, never scores or ranks people and never rejects anyone.
   - The HR Agent drafts, tracks and flags. It **never decides, never scores or ranks people, and never sends anything on its own.** Every draft waits for the employer's approval.
   - Never say the agent "handles it for you" or "takes care of" employees.
8. **No prices for the HR Agent.** It's early access, so the call to action is "Book a demo".
9. **No handles, no personal names, no mention of any employer** other than the fictional ones shown on screen.

### Facts you can use

- **Hiring tools:**
  - The first job is free, with no card needed.
  - Reference checks are $9 each with no subscription. The candidate gives consent first, and you get a PDF report.
  - Scoring guides for 78 roles. Free job description templates for 70 roles.
  - Prescreens go out by email, and answers come back in one place.
  - Candidates are compared side by side.
  - The onboarding checklist covers TD1, direct deposit and WHMIS.
- **HR Agent:**
  - Tracks probation ends, certification expiries, leave returns, anniversaries and last days.
  - Drafts letters written for the employee's province: probation extensions, warnings, offers, leave approvals and return-to-work plans.
  - Every draft goes to an approval queue, where you approve, edit or bin it.
  - Covers all 13 provinces and territories plus federally regulated employers.
  - Shows its sources and flags anything it isn't sure of.
  - Gives a final pay and ROE checklist when someone leaves.
  - Rules are updated when employment standards change.
  - Each company gets its own private install, with records stored in Canada.

- **Font:** Outfit (Google Fonts), sentence case always. Headlines 700, body 400–500.
- **Logo:** two linked rings (two people, one team) beside the lowercase wordmark "staffkin".
  - On light backgrounds the rings are indigo and periwinkle.
  - On indigo the left ring is white.
  - Never recolour, stretch or add effects to it.
- **End cards (already in every video, last 3–4 s):**
  - **Hiring:** indigo background, white and periwinkle rings, "Hire people, not paperwork.", a "Try for free" button, staffkin.com, and "No card needed · Made in Nova Scotia".
  - **HR Agent:** light background, rings, "Staffkin HR Agent", "You're there for your people. We handle the sheets and systems.", and a "Book a demo at staffkin.com/hr-agent" button.
- **Music:** royalty-free and cleared for business use (Staffkin posts from a business page). Keep it under the voice-over at about 15–20% volume.
  - **Hiring:** light and upbeat (lo-fi, acoustic or indie-pop). The POV reel can be quirky or comedic.
  - **HR Agent:** calm and warm (soft piano or ambient, 70–95 BPM), never EDM or hype.

## 6. Video rules

- **Format:** vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, MP4. The one exception is H-YOUTUBE, which is 16:9.
- **Face on camera:** none. Every video is motion graphics and product screens.
- **Voice-over:** yes on every video except H-01 (music and sound effects only) and H-MOTION (music only).
- **Captions:**
  - The headline captions are already burned into each video.
  - Don't add auto-captions or extra text over them.
  - HR-00 has its own subtitles.
- **Safe areas:** keep anything you add out of the top 220 px, the bottom 420 px and the right 120 px, where the app's buttons sit.
- **End card:** don't cover it or talk over its last second. The voice-over should say the call to action as the end card appears.
- **Sound effects:** subtle only, like soft pings, clicks and whooshes on scene changes.
