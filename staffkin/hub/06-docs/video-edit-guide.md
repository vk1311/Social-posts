# Staffkin videos: edit guide

This folder has 14 videos. Add audio in Edits, then post on the **Staffkin Facebook page**. Instagram can use the same files once it's connected.

## Before you start (applies to every video)

- **Music must be cleared for business use.** Staffkin is a business page, so popular chart songs can get muted or blocked there.
  - In Edits, pick tracks marked for business or commercial use.
  - Or use Meta Sound Collection (free to use on Facebook and Instagram).
  - Search by the mood words given for each video below.
- **The words are already on screen**, and most people watch without sound.
  - Voice-over should say the same thing as the captions, or less. Never different words.
  - Keep music low under a voice-over: about 15–20% volume.
- **Cut on the beat.** The "Scene changes" times below are where a beat drop or a whoosh sound fits.
- **Voice:** your own voice works well. Calm, friendly, not salesy. If you use a text-to-speech voice, pick a natural Canadian or North American one.
- **Don't cover the end card** (the last 3–4 seconds). It has the logo, the web address and the button.
- **Export:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Keep the file name, so I know which is which when you send them back.

## Staffkin hiring reels (folder `1-staffkin-hiring`)

### H-01 POV: hiring the old way (18.5 s)
- **Audio:** music only, no voice-over. It's the funny one.
- **Music:** playful, quirky, a little chaotic, 110–125 BPM. Search: *quirky*, *comedy*, *playful pizzicato*, *office funny*.
- **Sound effects:**
  - notification pings at 3 s, when the alerts stack up;
  - a phone buzz on each voicemail bubble (7.5–10.5 s);
  - a record scratch or a "boing" when "Reference check?" gets crossed out (~10.5 s);
  - a calm reset or whoosh at "Or. One link." (12 s).
- **Scene changes:** 3 s, 4.5 s, 7.5 s, 10.5 s, 12 s, and 15 s (end card).
- **Tip:** make the music stop dead at 12 s, then switch to a calmer track for "One link". The contrast is the joke.

### H-06 Scoring in 10 seconds (15 s)
- **Audio:** voice-over plus light music.
- **Music:** light lo-fi or upbeat acoustic, 90–100 BPM. Search: *lo-fi*, *light corporate*, *acoustic positive*.
- **Voice-over (≈ 13 s):**
  - 0.3 s: "How to score an interview answer in ten seconds."
  - 3 s: "One. Read the guide."
  - 6 s: "Two. Write what they actually did."
  - 7.5 s: "Three. Score it against the guide."
  - 10 s: "Four. Average it, and compare."
  - 12.5 s: "Scoring guides for 78 roles. Free at staffkin.com."
- **Sound effect:** a soft click at ~9 s, when the "4" gets tapped.

### H-10 Your first job is free (14 s)
- **Audio:** music, with an optional short voice-over.
- **Music:** upbeat indie-pop or feel-good, 115–125 BPM. Search: *upbeat*, *feel good*, *happy indie*.
- **Scene changes:** each checklist tick lands on a beat, from 3 s to 10.5 s (7 ticks, about 1 per second). End card at 10.5 s.
- **Voice-over (optional):**
  - 0.3 s: "Your first job on Staffkin is free."
  - 3 s: "Job description, prescreen, interview guide, PDFs, side-by-side scores, onboarding. All in."
  - 10.5 s: "No card needed. Staffkin.com."

### H-MOTION Staffkin in 26 seconds (26 s)
- **Audio:** music only.
- **Music:** confident, modern corporate or indie-electronic, 100–120 BPM, with a clear drop. Search: *tech product*, *modern corporate*, *upbeat electronic*.
- **Scene changes:**
  - 0 s: "Hiring a line cook?", with a paper-rustle sound;
  - 3 s: the logo appears. Put the beat drop here.
  - 4.5 s: One job, everything ready.
  - 9 s: Prescreens.
  - 12 s: Scoring.

  - 21 s: End card.

## Staffkin HR Agent (folder `2-staffkin-hr-agent`)

- **Same mood for all of these:** calm, warm, trustworthy. The feeling is "someone's got your back". Avoid hype or EDM.
- **Music search:** *calm piano*, *soft ambient*, *gentle corporate*, *hopeful*, 70–95 BPM.
- **Structure:** each one is 18 s. Three captions run from 0 to 15 s, then the end card runs to 18 s ("Book a demo at staffkin.com/hr-agent").
- **Voice-over:** in each script below, the timings show when each line starts, and the voice-over reads the captions word for word.
- **Rule:** never say the agent "decides", "sends" or "handles it for you". It **drafts, tracks and flags**, and you approve.

### HR-00 Meet your HR Agent: the ring character (36 s)
- **Voice-over is required.** The mouth moves on these exact timings.
  - 0.6 s: "Hi, I'm your Staffkin HR Agent."
  - 4.6 s: "You're there for your people. I handle the sheets and systems."
  - 9.8 s: "Overnight, I check every deadline for your whole team."
  - 14.6 s: "I draft the letters, written for each employee's province."
  - 19.8 s: "By morning, it's all waiting for your approval."
  - 24.6 s: "And I never send anything without you."
  - 29.0 s: "Book a demo at staffkin.com/hr-agent."
- **Voice:** friendly, a little playful, like a helpful assistant. In Edits you can pitch it up slightly for a character feel.
- **Music:** soft and bouncy, very low (10–15%).

### HR-01 You're there for your people
- **Voice-over:**
  - 0.3 s: "Your job is people."
  - 5.2 s: "Not spreadsheets, deadlines and forms."
  - 10.2 s: "You're there for your people. We handle the sheets and systems."
- **Music:** warm piano. Let it swell a little at 10 s.

### HR-02 Letters on deadline
- **Voice-over:**
  - 0.3 s: "Probation ends Friday."
  - 4.4 s: "Your agent drafts the letter overnight."
  - 9.0 s: "It waits for your approval. Nothing sends without you."
- **Sound effects:** soft typing from 4.4 to 8.8 s, and a gentle "ding" when Approve is tapped (~12 s).

### HR-03 Every HR date
- **Voice-over:**
  - 0.3 s: "Every HR date. Tracked."
  - 4.6 s: "Probation ends. Certifications expire. People come back from leave."
  - 10.1 s: "You hear about it before it's due."
- **Sound effects:** a soft tick as each calendar dot appears (4.6–9.9 s).

### HR-04 Works overnight
- **Voice-over:**
  - 0.3 s: "Six p.m. You go home."
  - 4.2 s: "Your agent keeps working."
  - 11.0 s: "Seven a.m. Ready for your approval."
- **Music:** night-time lo-fi or ambient (search *night lo-fi*, *dreamy*). Brighten it at 11 s when it turns to morning. A light "morning chime" at 11 s fits well.

### HR-05 Every province
- **Voice-over:**
  - 0.3 s: "HR rules differ across Canada."
  - 4.2 s: "Same question. The right answer for where they work."
  - 10.1 s: "All thirteen provinces and territories. Plus federal."

### HR-06 Last day, sorted
- **Voice-over:**
  - 0.3 s: "Someone's last day is coming."
  - 4.4 s: "Your agent builds the checklist."
  - 11.0 s: "Sorted before Friday."
- **Sound effects:** a soft check sound on each ticked row (4.4–10.8 s).

### HR-07 Burnout is real
- **Voice-over:** slower and softer than the others.
  - 0.3 s: "Burnout is real in HR."
  - 6.0 s: "Forms. Deadlines. Rule changes. All on you."
  - 11.0 s: "Hand the paperwork to your agent."
- **Music:** gentle, slightly emotional piano. Let it lift at 11 s.
- **Caption idea:** you can add the stat in the post text: "54% of HR professionals burned out in the past five years (Personio survey via People Management, Jan 2025)". Keep the source.

### HR-08 Wording and law changes
- **Voice-over:**
  - 0.3 s: "Not sure how to word it?"
  - 5.0 s: "Get a clear, professional draft."
  - 9.6 s: "Law changed? We update the rules."
