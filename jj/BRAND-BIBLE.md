## 2. Logo

A white serif italic **"JJ"** monogram inside a sky-blue circle, framed by a laurel wreath, with **"Jay Jalaram Pumps & Spares"** in Poppins Bold underneath.

| File (brand/logos/) | What it is | Use on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jj-logo-full-white-bg.png | Full logo, navy wordmark | White and light backgrounds |
| jj-logo-full-navy-bg.png | Full logo, light-blue wordmark on Deep Navy | Dark panels, covers, end cards |
| jj-logo-full-black-bg.png | Full logo, light-blue wordmark on black | Night / festival designs |
| jj-mark-white-bg.png | Monogram and wreath only, on white | Profile pictures, small spaces |
| jj-mark-transparent.png | Monogram and wreath only, transparent | Footers, watermarks on dark backgrounds |

**Rules**

- Never recolour, stretch, crop, rotate or add effects to the logo.
- Keep clear space around it at least the height of one "J".
- On photos, place it on a solid panel (white or Deep Navy), never directly on a busy image.
- Minimum size: 64 px tall on screen.

## 5. Voice and tone

Sound like an experienced pump mechanic explaining something useful to a customer: calm, confident, practical and warm. Say what the pump does and what the customer should do. No drama.

**Tone rules**

- Grounded and factual. Plain, everyday words.
- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Helpful first, selling second: tips, checklists and explanations earn the right to ask for the call.
- Respectful and warm for festival greetings, with no sales pitch beyond the contact line.

**Language**

| Channel | Language |
| --- | --- |
| Social post images and captions | English (for now) |
| Reel voiceover | Hinglish — Hindi with common English words (pump, motor, borewell, starter, spares) |
| Reel on-screen captions | Hinglish in Roman script, word for word with the voiceover |

**Key messages** (use these, in your own words)

1. We make our pumps ourselves, in our own workshop in Ahmedabad.
2. Our motors have copper rotors.
3. A full V6 range: Mixflow, K-Type, 262, 273, NENO, 50 Feet and AR series, plus motors, pump-only and spares.
4. We help you choose the right pump for your borewell — tell us the borewell size, water depth and field size.
5. We also repair pumps and take job work.

**Words to avoid**

- "Best", "No. 1", "India's leading", "world-class", "unbeatable", "premium quality" as a claim.
- "Guaranteed", "lifetime", "never fails".
- Engagement bait: "comment YES", "tag a friend", "share if you agree", "limited offer", "hurry".

**Claims rules**

- Only two product claims are approved: **copper rotor motors** and **manufactured in-house**.
- No ISI / BIS certification claims. No warranty claims.
- No invented numbers: no specs, speeds, prices, years in business, customer counts or percentages unless Jay Jalaram Pumps confirms them.
- No testimonials or customer quotes unless they are real and permission is given.
- No competitor names or comparisons.
- Never show or read out other brand names on pump nameplates — hide them in photos and video.
- No prices in marketing materials.

## 8. Visual style

- **Clean and premium.** Real product photos of our own pumps on white or softly faded backgrounds.
- **Never cartoonish or AI-looking.** No drawn scenery, no AI-generated people, no emoji, no clip-art.
- **Backgrounds:** farm, water, river and hill photos, faded to about 20–40% so they sit behind the pumps. Festival posts may use a full diya photo.
- **Pumps:** cut out cleanly with a soft floor shadow. Nameplates of other brands hidden.
- **Layout:** headline top-left, pump on the right, Deep Navy footer with logo, full name, phone and handle.
- **Carousel inner slides:** white cards with a thin Line border on a soft background; final slide Deep Navy with the WhatsApp number in a Sky Blue pill.

**Post footer** (every single post and carousel slide)

Logo · Jay Jalaram Pumps & Spares · JJ Pumps · Bakrol, Ahmedabad · +91 92271 06108 · @jayjalarampumps
