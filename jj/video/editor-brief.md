## 3. Voice and tone

Calm, confident, practical and warm, like an experienced pump mechanic explaining something useful across the counter.

- **Language:** Hinglish.
- **Voice:** male, mature (35–50), clear and unhurried.
- **Words:** simple and spoken. Explain any technical term in the same sentence.
- **Structure:** hook → story → hook → story, about 50 words for a 21-second reel.
- **Closing line:** "Call ya WhatsApp karein: 92271 06108" then "Jay Jalaram Pumps. Water Power You Can Depend On."

**Facts you may state:** our motors have copper rotors; we manufacture our pumps ourselves in our own Ahmedabad workshop; the product and service names above.

**Hard rules**

1. No facts beyond the list above. No numbers, specs, speeds, prices, years, customer counts or percentages unless the per-video notes give them.
2. No testimonials or customer quotes.
3. No ISI / BIS certification and no warranty promises.
4. No hype: never "best", "No. 1", "India's leading" or "world-class".
5. No engagement bait or fake urgency.
6. Never mention Swajal or any other brand name; never read a pump nameplate.
7. No competitor names or comparisons.
8. Festival videos: warm greeting, no sales pitch beyond the closing line.
