## What it is
**Jay Jalaram Pumps & Spares (JJ Pumps)**, the family pump business in Rakhial, Ahmedabad, India. It makes V6 submersible pump sets (Mixflow, K-type, SS 50 ft, NENO / Heavy series), V6 motors (Full SS and Carbon Bearing), openwell pumps and spares, and sells to farmers and borewell owners.
- **WhatsApp / phone:** +91 92271 06108
- **Website:** jayjalarampumps.in
- **Social:** Facebook and Instagram via Postiz; also an IndiaMART catalogue.

## Voice
Warm, respectful and practical: "Namaste!", "Jay Mataji!" on festival days, and advice-first posts (which pump fits your borewell, 5 signs your pump needs a check-up, copper rotor versus aluminium). The call to action is always "Send us 3 things on WhatsApp" (borewell size, water depth, fields to water). The posting calendar follows Indian festivals and crop seasons (Navratri, Dussehra, Dhanteras, Diwali, New Year / Bhai Dooj, rabi sowing).
